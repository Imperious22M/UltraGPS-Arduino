Hardware¶

Both stations are Arduino Unos (ATmega328P at 16 MHz) with an nRF24L01+ radio module and five status LEDs. The pin assignments are identical apart from the radio IRQ line, which only the transmitter station uses.

Bill of materials¶

Item Qty Notes
Arduino Uno 2 One per station
nRF24L01+ radio module 2 2.4 GHz, SPI
HC-SR04-style ultrasonic transmitter 1 Driven by the transmitter station
Ultrasonic receivers 6 Echo lines feed the base station's A0–A5
Status LEDs (+ resistors, optional) 10 Five per station

Power the radio properly

The nRF24L01+ draws current in bursts that the Uno's 3.3 V regulator handles poorly. A 10 µF capacitor across the module's VCC/GND is the usual fix for radio.begin() succeeding but packets failing intermittently.

4 OUT STAT_2 LED "

6 OUT STAT_3 LED LedControl
7 OUT PULSE_TRIG LED — lit while a pulse is in flight "
8 OUT RADIO_STATUS LED — solid once the radio initialises "

A0–A5 IN (pull-up) Echo lines from receivers 0–5 ECHO_PIN[]

The six echo lines must stay on A0–A5: measure_echo_times() reads them as a group with PINC & 63, which is a direct read of PORTC's low six bits. Moving a receiver to a digital pin would silently drop it from the measurement.

Pin 2 must be interrupt-capable.

Radio link¶

Both stations configure the nRF24L01+ identically in RadioController::init(), except for which pipe they write to:

Setting Value
Channel 100
Data rate RF24_1MBPS
PA level RF24_PA_HIGH
Payload 8 bytes, fixed (dynamic payloads disabled)
Auto-ack Off
Retries setRetries(0, 0) — none
Base station writes to PIPE_TX_TO_RX (0xF0F0F0F0E1)
Transmitter listens on PIPE_TX_TO_RX, writes to PIPE_RX_TO_TX (0xF0F0F0F0E2)

Auto-ack and retries are deliberately off: a late trigger packet is worse than a lost one, so the link is fire-and-forget.

The 8-byte payload is CommsPacket (radio.h):

Field Type Meaning
type PacketType PKT_NO_DATA (1) or PKT_TRIGGER (2)
_pad[3] uint8_t Alignment padding to keep the struct at 8 bytes
timestamp uint16_t The base station's TCNT1 at send time
tick_delay uint16_t Microseconds the transmitter waits before firing

Status LEDs¶

LedControl drives all five LEDs on each board and holds a shadow copy of their state, so getStat1() and friends never need a digitalRead on an output pin.

LED Meaning
RADIO_STATUS (pin 8) Solid once the radio initialises. Blinking on a two-second cycle means radio init failed and the sketch has halted.
PULSE_TRIG (pin 7) Lit while a trigger is in active
STAT_1–STAT_3 (pins 3, 4, 6) Reserved — wired and controllable, but nothing currently drives them

Both sketches call leds.init(0), which sets the pins to output and drives them low without the startup animation. Pass leds.init(1) (or omit the argument) to run startupAnimation() — a chase pattern across all five LEDs, useful as a quick "the board rebooted" indicator.
